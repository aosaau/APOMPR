# OPN™ Global Intelligence Architecture

## The Geological Moat
The true defensibility of the Opal Provenance Network (OPN™) is not built on blockchain—it is built on **trust, standardization, geological intelligence, historical permanence, and network effects**. By establishing absolute clarity in a fractured market, OPN becomes the de facto layer of truth for geological rarity. The following outlines the roadmap and infrastructure for the future intelligence layer.

---

## 1. Geological Fingerprinting Engine

The goal is to render every geological asset uniquely and irrefutably identifiable, preventing forgery and tracking assets across their entire lifecycle without relying on physical tagging.

*   **Micro-Topographical Mapping**: High-resolution image ingestion maps the exact surface contours and microscopic pit structures of the stone.
*   **Spectral Uniqueness Scoring (SUS)**: AI models analyze color play, light refraction angles, and pattern density (e.g., Harlequin vs. Pinfire) to generate a cryptographic geological hash.
*   **Visual Identity Matching**: A regression model that can take a low-quality smartphone photo from a secondary market (like eBay) and match it against the high-resolution vault twin with >99% confidence.
*   **Teachable Machine Integration**: The Andamooka standard is codified via `https://teachablemachine.withgoogle.com/models/jOWvy-EIT/`, forming the baseline for edge-based M1-M9 classification.

---

## 2. Market Intelligence Engine

Moving from subjective approximations to a "Bloomberg Terminal for Earth’s Assets," this engine provides real-time financialization metrics.

*   **Valuation Confidence Scoring**: AI model that assesses liquidity, historical comps, and current macroeconomic indicators to provide a real-time Net Asset Value (NAV) with a ±% confidence interval.
*   **Rarity Trend Analysis**: Tracking market sentiment towards specific spectral patterns (e.g., "Are blue-green ribbon patterns trading at a premium this quarter?").
*   **Miner vs. Cutter Variance Index**: Big data analysis tracking the delta between rough parcel prices at the mine shaft vs. fully realized gem value, highlighting market inefficiencies and arbitrage opportunities for investors.
*   **Automated Liquidity Forecasting**: Predicting the average days-on-market for an asset based on its M-grade and current demand metrics.

---

## 3. The Provenance Graph

A deeply interconnected data structure linking objects, geography, and humans across time.

*   **Geographic Extraction History**: Mapping stones back to specific seams and longitudinal coordinates in the Andamooka region, allowing for "Terroir-based" valuations (similar to fine wine).
*   **Ownership Lineage Mapping**: A chronological, immutable ledger of hands the stone has passed through.
*   **Authenticity Confidence Level**: A dynamic score reflecting the unbroken chain of custody. A stone mined, graded by VerifyAI™, and locked in OPN immediately scores 100%. A legacy stone onboarded later requires multi-node verification.

---

## 4. Collector Reputation System

Gamifying and incentivizing positive participation in the ecosystem.

*   **Vault Prestige Scoring**: An aggregated metric of a collector’s holdings based on cumulative M-grades, rarity percentiles, and market value.
*   **Collector Rarity Index**: A badge system for collectors who assemble specific sets (e.g., holding M9 grades across three different pattern types).
*   **Provenance Contribution Score**: Rewarding users (curators, cutters, original miners) who enrich the metadata of an asset, ensuring the ledger remains historically vibrant.

The infrastructure designed for Australian Matrix Opals is highly scalable. The M1-M9 verification protocol serves as a blueprint for the financialization of other opaque, subjective asset classes.

1.  **Sapphires & Rubies**: Implementing inclusion mapping and heat-treatment detection AI.

5.  **Luxury Geological Artifacts**: Historical carvings and heritage artifacts where provenance is the primary driver of value.
